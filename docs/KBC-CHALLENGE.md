# KBC Challenge

KBC is our first case partner. They are one of the
largest bank in Belgium, providing banking,
investment, and insurance services.

## The Challenge

Imagine a future where KBC perfectly understands
what customers need and responds at exactly the
right moment.

First, think without constraints. What would the ideal
customer experience look like? Then, explore how
that experience could be delivered to more than
2,300,000 customers in a scalable way.

We challenge you not to create just another feature,
but to build a proof of concept for a new way in which
KBC understands, supports, and guides its
customers.

Consider questions such as:

1. What signals can help us understand what
   customers need?
2. How can customers be recognized based on their
   situation, behavior, and intent?
3. How can personalized experiences automatically
   adapt to each customer?
4. How can this work seamlessly across products,
   services, and channels?
5. How can you create meaningful impact for millions
   of customers at the same time?

## What We're Looking For

We're not looking for a new feature.

We're looking for a vision and a proof of concept for a
scalable personalization approach that fundamentally
strengthens the relationship between KBC and its
customers.

Think big. Challenge assumptions. Reimagine how
a bank can understand, support, and guide
customers at
scale.
